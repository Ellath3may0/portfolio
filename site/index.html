Live Music Mixing 2026

Folklorico

  • Yamaha DM7
  • NEXO PA
  • Latin Music

This is a live music performance by a local Latin band, for which I designed the sound system and mixed the show. I used a Yamaha DM7 digital mixer to manage the inputs and effects, and a NEXO PA system to ensure clear, powerful sound throughout the venue. Systems Administration 2025 (Ongoing)

Homelab

  • Tailscale
  • Docker
  • Linux

I have been cultivating a homelab for a little over a year now. I currently have an HP workstation computer running Ubuntu Server, hosting a variety of services for both fun and practical use. Some highlights include a Jellyfin media server filled with content I have ripped from my own collection, a Nextcloud instance for collabourative file storage and syncing, and a vaultwarden instance for secure password management. I have primarily used a Tailscale network to securely access my homelab from anywhere as well as provide access to my services to users, and I am in the process of migrating everything to proxmox, as well as implementing a solution for high availability and failover.
The landing page itself I made in an hour for fun one afternoon. I do eventually intend to improve upon many aspects of the system, but I have more learning to do to get there!

Network Design 2026

Team 5 Consulting

  • Network Design
  • Business Cost-Awareness
  • Security & Compliance

This was a made-up IT consulting firm I was a part of for my network systems & design class. We were tasked with designing a few different responses to requests for proposals, from companies of a small scale like a jewelry store to larger scales including a school district, a hospital, and an international corporation. We had to design solutions for their networking needs, including hardware recommendations, and software-defined WAN solutions, service consolidation, and security measures, and create comprehensive proposal with detailed descriptions of each of our solutions.
I also was tasked specifically on our team with maintaining a consistent brand image across our proposals, which you can see the result of in this project's thumbnail.
