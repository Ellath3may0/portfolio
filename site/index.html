01 — About

I grew up as a military kid, moving around a lot and picking up a wide range of interests and hobbies along the way. I started programming in high school and quickly found myself drawn to backend development and network engineering. It was during this time I also took up sound engineering, starting in small venues and now designing systems and mixing bands weekly.

I am passionate about learning everything I can. Notably, how to get the most out of what I have, whether that's pushing a PA system to its limits, cutting off every bottleneck on a network, or optimising code to run at light speed on a toaster. I want to work with people who are just as excited about solving problems as I am, and who value creativity and resourcefulness just as much.
